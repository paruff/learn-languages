# Phase 1 Learner Session Protocol

The one remaining blocker on #20's Plan: "direct observational evidence for or against 'learners return after session 1'" (discovery-brief.md R3). This can't be produced from a coding session — it needs a human to run real sessions with real people and report back. This doc exists so that person doesn't have to figure out what to do from scratch.

## Who

3-5 people matching a discovery-brief persona (§3):
- **Commuter Carlos** — uses dead time, offline, short sessions
- **Fragmented-Time Fiona** — 5-10 min bursts, needs instant resume, no onboarding

Don't recruit people who already know Portuguese — the point is testing whether an A1 beginner can use the review loop, not whether the content is accurate.

## What to give them

The review page: `/en-GB/pt-PT/review/`. Say nothing else — no walkthrough, no explanation of SRS or CEFR. If they need instructions to get started, that's a finding, not a setup step to fix first.

## What to observe (session 1)

- Do they understand what to do without being told? (reveal → grade)
- Where do they hesitate or ask "what does this button do"?
- Do they finish the 10-card session, or abandon partway? If they abandon, at what point?
- Any confusion about the four grade buttons (Again/Hard/Good/Easy) — is the distinction between Hard and Again clear?

## What to record

| Learner | Persona match | Completed session? | Abandoned at | Notable friction | Returned day 2? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | |

Fill the last column a day or two later — that's the actual R3 signal (discovery-brief AC / success metric: 40% week-2 return rate is the 3-month target, but even "did they come back at all the next day" is the earliest real data point).

## What counts as a result

This isn't pass/fail against a number — 3-5 people is a signal, not a statistically valid sample. The question the Plan (#20) needs answered:

- **Clear positive**: most people finish session 1 without confusion and at least some come back. → Proceed with Phase 2/3 as currently specced.
- **Clear negative**: people bounce off before finishing, or nobody returns. → Don't build more content/UI on top of this loop yet — the friction is worth fixing first, and #20 should be rescoped based on what specifically broke.
- **Mixed/unclear**: normal with n=3-5. Note what was ambiguous and whether a slightly larger or more targeted round would resolve it.
