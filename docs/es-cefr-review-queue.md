# es-ES B1–C2 review queue (#185)

Item-by-item audit of every es-ES B1–C2 vocabulary item against its concept, its gloss and its example. It lists what was corrected and every item the audit was not sure of. **The audit was done by an AI model on 2026-11-12, without access to the PCIC inventories. It is not the PCIC review that #185 and [`es-cefr-mapping.md`](es-cefr-mapping.md) require**; it is the worklist for that review.

## Scope and method

- 1,038 items in 100 files (`src/content/realisations/es-ES/{b1,b2,c1,c2}-*.yaml`): 600 keyed to a shared en-GB concept, 438 older items whose concept exists only in es-ES.
- Each item was read for four things: the `term` has the sense of the `concept`; `translation` is the single en-GB term; `exampleTranslation` translates the Spanish `example`; the Spanish is correct and plausible for Spain.
- No positional drift of the kind reverted in #155 was found: in all 600 shared items the term matches the concept's sense or is flagged below.
- Items not listed here read as correct to the auditor. That is an AI opinion, not a sign-off.

Each flagged item also carries a `# REVIEW #185 (A|B|C): …` comment above it in its YAML file. Remove the comment and the row here when the item is resolved.

| Severity | Means                                                                                      | Count |

| B        | Doubt about sense, register or Spain usage. A Spanish speaker should decide                | 33    |
| C        | Minor: a literal rendering, an unnatural example, or a note                                | 39    |
